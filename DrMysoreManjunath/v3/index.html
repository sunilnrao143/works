My Journey

Rooted in Mysore. Reaching the World.

Sumanth Manjunath represents the fourth generation of the illustrious Mysore violin lineage. Trained from a young age under the guidance of his grandfather, veteran violinist Sri Mahadevappa, his uncle — Violin Maestro Vidwan Mysore Nagaraj — and his father, globally acclaimed Violin Maestro Dr. Mysore Manjunath.

A gold medallist in Masters of Music from the University of Mysore, he is also a passionate educator and cultural ambassador of India.

He has performed in more than 25 countries, building bridges between Indian classical music and diverse global traditions through concerts, collaborations and workshops.
