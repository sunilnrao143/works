The Maestro

Born into music, chosen by destiny

The garland he wasnever given

As a boy, Manjunath was the one member of Mysore's most celebrated musical household who felt no pull towards music at all.

His father, Vidwan Prof. S. Mahadevappa, and his elder brother Mysore Nagaraj were established violinists. The family breathed Carnatic music. And yet he remained unmoved — until one evening after a concert, when the whole ensemble was garlanded on stage and the boy who had sat quietly behind his father for four hours received nothing. The organiser made him a promise: perform next year, and a garland would be waiting.

That single moment of wounded pride began one of the most extraordinary careers in the history of Indian classical music.

My brother and I used to practise eight to ten hours a day. If we didn't practise in the morning, there was no breakfast. If we didn't practise in the evening, there was no dinner. Dr. Mysore Manjunath

In the early 1990s, a young Indian violinist walked into Princeton University wearing a dhoti and sat cross-legged on the floor with his violin. The next morning, every Western violinist at the workshop arrived in a dhoti and sat on the floor. That is the power of a tradition embodied completely.

From the Royal Albert Hall to the Sydney Opera House, from the World Music Festival in Chicago to the Esplanade in Singapore, he has performed across more than 53 countries. Today he serves as National President of Samskara Bharathi, one of India's largest cultural organisations, continuing the work of preserving and carrying forward the country's classical heritage.

Compositions and creations

New ragas,new anthems

Yoga Anthem · 2019 · Raga Bharata

Yoga Niyoga

India's Yoga Anthem, commissioned by the Centre for Soft Power. Five minutes, 20+ Indian and international artists, composed in the brand-new Raga Bharata — named after India's legendary first emperor. Released at the British Parliament on World Yoga Day, 21 June 2019.

Explore Yoga Niyoga →

Pandemic Response · 2020 · Raga Charukeshi

Life Again

A global humanitarian response to COVID-19, bringing together twenty musicians from the worst-affected nations — including Wang Ying from Wuhan. Launched by the ICCR in 150+ countries, with lyrics by Sri Ganapathi Sachchidananda Swami. “A time for social distancing, not emotional distancing.”

Watch Life Again →

Music-Play · 2022 · Ranga Shankara, Bengaluru

Aaroha

A music-play composed and directed for Bhoomija Trust, built around a 20-member youth orchestra performing original work. The press called it “a perfect synchrony with Carnatic idioms and western presentation.” With S. Surendranath, Srinivasa Prabhu and S.G. Vasudev.

Raga Creation · Ongoing

New Ragas

Raga Yaduveera Manohari, composed for the Mysore royal family's wedding in June 2016, and Raga Bharata — both verified by scholars as genuinely new melodic personalities. Creating a raga remains among the rarest achievements in all of Carnatic music.
