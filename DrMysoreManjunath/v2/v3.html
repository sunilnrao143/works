The Lineage

Preserving the Authentic Mysore Bani

Rooted in the royal musical courts of the Wodeyar dynasty, the Mysore tradition (Mysore Bani) is defined by deep melodic phrasing, complex gamakas (ornaments), and strict adherence to classical raga grammar.

Trained under his direct Guru and father, Vidwan Prof. S. Mahadevappa, Dr. Manjunath represents the unbroken continuation of three generations of musical genius. Alongside his brother Mysore Nagaraj, "The Mysore Brothers" duo has elevated Carnatic violin duets onto world-renowned platforms.
